Tether

Watch the same show at the same time, apart. Press play and it plays for both of you, on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Max, Crunchyroll, and YouTube.

Add to Chrome

Free. No sign-up.

How it works

  1. You both add Tether to Chrome.
  2. Open a show and click Watch together on the right side of the video. Your invite link is copied.
  3. Send them the link. They land on the same show at the same moment, and from then on, play, pause, and skip happen on both screens.

Good to know

  • You each watch with your own streaming account.
  • Chat and reactions stay on the video, even in fullscreen, and out of the way of the player's controls.
  • Nothing about what you watch is shared until you start a session. It ends when you say so, or after 30 minutes without a video.
