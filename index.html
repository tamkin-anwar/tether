Tether

Keeps Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Max, and YouTube in sync between two people, anywhere, plus a shared notes pad, chat, and reactions.

Add to Chrome

Free, on the Chrome Web Store, installs in one click.

Setup (about a minute)

  1. Click the button above, then Add to Chrome.
  2. If you were sent an invite link, click it, you'll land right in the room, on the exact show, picked up right where they are, once they've pressed play. Sent a code instead? Click the Tether icon, paste it under "Join someone else's room", and click Join.
  3. Open Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Max, or YouTube and press play, it'll stay in sync from there.
