The simple, private way to have a watch party with someone far away.

Tether keeps the streaming services you already use in perfect sync between two people, wherever you both are. Press play, pause, or skip ahead, and it happens on both screens within a fraction of a second. No video call, no screen sharing, and no counting down "3, 2, 1, play."

How it works
• Send your invite link once. One click brings the other person to the same show, at the same moment.
• Open a show and click Watch together. They get an invite on their screen and join with one tap.
• Each of you watches with your own account, in your own browser.

Why Tether
• Stays in sync. Tether quietly corrects any drift, even across countries and time zones.
• Works with ad-supported plans. Tether waits out ad breaks and brings you back together when they end, even if you each see different ads.
• Keeps playing when you switch tabs or windows.
• Free, with no account and no sign-up.

Built in
• Chat on the video itself, even in fullscreen.
• Emoji reactions that float over the video without interrupting it.
• See who did what, like "your partner paused" or "skipped ahead."
• If they move to another episode, one click takes you there too.
• Playback speed stays the same for both of you.
• Shared notes for what to watch next.
• Available in English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, हिन्दी, and 日本語.

Private by design
Tether only shares anything while you're watching together, and you always choose when to start or join. A session ends when you end it, or on its own after 30 minutes without a video. Your room can only be opened with its code or invite link. Tether never accesses the video itself, only playback timing, and you can delete your chat and notes at any time.

Made for long-distance couples, friends, and family who want to watch together from anywhere.
